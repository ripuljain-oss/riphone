On the quietness of tools ★

There is a kind of software that announces itself constantly. Every action is celebrated; every save is a confetti cannon. I am suspicious of it.

The good tools — the ones I keep using year after year — are the quiet ones. They do their work and then they recede. You do not notice them at all until the moment you need them, and then they are exactly where you left them.

A tool is not your friend. A tool is the silence between your hands and the thing you are trying to make.

When a piece of software starts talking, it is usually trying to sell you something. Look closely. The most expensive subscription is the one you forget you are paying for.
