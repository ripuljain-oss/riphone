One sponsor per week.
No banners. No tracking.

ripblog has a small, deliberate audience — about 38,000 weekly readers who care about how software is made and how it reads. They are designers, engineers, and people who still keep a folder of .txt files on their desktop. They open every newsletter. They click links.

I sell one sponsorship per week. The sponsor gets a single, plainly-marked entry at the top of the feed — a short paragraph, written in my voice, about a product I would actually use. There is no banner. There is no tracking pixel. There is no popup. There never will be.

“The most expensive thing on the internet is your reader's patience. Don't spend it on something that isn't yours.”
